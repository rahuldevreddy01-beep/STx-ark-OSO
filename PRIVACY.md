# Privacy Policy for STx-ARK OSO

**Last Updated:** November 22, 2026

Welcome to **STx-ARK OSO** ("we", "our", or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how our Android application collects, uses, and safeguards your information when you use our launcher and assistant application.

### 1. Information We Collect
To provide an immersive HUD launcher experience and AI assistant capabilities, STx-ARK OSO may access or collect the following types of information:
* **Device Telemetry & Sensors:** Battery status, network connection type (Wi-Fi/cellular), RAM usage, step count, and compass/azimuth sensor data solely to display live HUD metrics on your dashboard.
* **Microphone Data:** Audio recorded locally when you explicitly issue voice commands or interact with the STX AI voice assistant. Audio is transmitted securely to cloud AI models for speech-to-text processing and command execution.
* **Camera Access:** Used exclusively when you open the in-app Camera HUD module or optical scanner features. Images are processed locally or within secure AI processing pipelines when requested by you.
* **Location Data:** Approximate or precise location data if granted, used solely for compass and environmental telemetry widgets.

### 2. How We Use Your Information
We use the collected information for the following purposes:
* To operate, maintain, and render the STx-ARK OSO custom launcher interface and HUD widgets.
* To process voice and text queries via **Firebase AI Logic / Google Gemini API** to generate intelligent assistant responses and execute tactical app shortcuts.
* To manage system-wide floating window overlays and device administrative settings requested by you.

### 3. Third-Party Services
STx-ARK OSO integrates with secure cloud infrastructure:
* **Firebase AI Logic (Google LLC):** Used to power cloud-based AI assistant responses and secure API communications with App Check attestation.

### 4. Data Security & Retention
We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal data to third parties. All data transmissions between the app and cloud AI services are encrypted using industry-standard HTTPS/TLS protocols. 

### 5. Children's Privacy
Our app does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13.

### 6. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.

### 7. Contact Us
If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us via your GitHub repository issues or developer profile.
